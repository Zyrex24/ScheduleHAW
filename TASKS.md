## Completion discipline

Mark tasks only after their exit checks pass. Keep source uncertainties as data issues, never convert them into invented rules. Do not claim all groups exhaustively checked when the node budget was reached. Do not mark T22 real-format coverage complete using only mocked/synthetic extraction. Do not mark optional backend deployment complete without rate-limit/retention/privacy controls.

Exact sequential execution and final definition of done are in [IMPLEMENTATION HANDOFF FOR SOL](docs/IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md#implementation-handoff-for-sol).
